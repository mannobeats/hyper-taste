Use the design-system skill to match the look of: $ARGUMENTS

1. Open it with the browser or computer use your harness gives you. Take screenshots at its main sizes, light and dark, and measure colors, fonts, sizes, radii, shadows, spacing and motion timings (computed styles on the web).
2. Decide whether it is the user's own brand or someone else's product used as a reference.
   - Own brand: apply its values exactly, as tokens in the project's format.
   - Someone else's: take the craft (type scale, radii, spacing, materials, motion timings), keep this product's content, accent and signature, and write what you took into `DESIGN.md`. Never take a logo, copy, icons or images.
3. Look at the result beside the reference at every size, light and dark.
