Use the ui-director skill to match the design of: $ARGUMENTS

1. Open the site with the browser your harness gives you (`references/look.md`). Take screenshots at desktop and phone widths, then read the computed styles of the body, headings, buttons, links, inputs and cards: colors, fonts, sizes, radii, shadows, spacing, and transition durations and easings.
2. Decide whether this is the user's own brand or someone else's site used as a blueprint.
   - Own brand: apply its colors, fonts and radii exactly, as the token knobs in the project's own format (`references/system.md`).
   - Someone else's site: write it into the "Blueprint" line of the direction in `DESIGN.md` (`references/landing.md`): what you take (how it shows the product, its materials, type scale, radii, motion timings) and what stays this product's (content, accent, signature). Never take the logo, copy or images.
3. Recreate only the motion that explains something, with the durations and easings you measured.
4. Check the result in your browser at both widths, in light and dark, with the page check from `references/look.md`.
