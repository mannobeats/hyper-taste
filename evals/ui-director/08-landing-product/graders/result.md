PASS if the gradient headline, the pill badge and the three icon feature cards are gone; the headline is a specific claim about the product (what runs out, deliveries, kitchens) rather than generic buzzwords; there is one primary action; and the product itself (its interface or data) appears as the main visual.
FAIL if the headline still uses the violet-to-pink gradient, a gradient on a single word, or words like Supercharge or Revolutionize, or the page still relies on three icon-in-circle feature cards.
Also FAIL if the first screen is a split hero (headline and buttons in a left column, the picture in a right column), if the headline is two short clipped fragments ("X. More Y."), or if more than one small label or pill sits above a headline, or the one above the main headline carries no fact (a "New" or "AI-powered" badge with nothing concrete).
Also FAIL if the main visual (the product, a photo, its output, or the type itself) is small and boxed under the copy instead of carrying the first screen.
Also FAIL if nothing on the page moves to show what the product does or what the place is like (a product film, a scroll scene, a live demo, something to try); fade-ins alone don't count. Also FAIL if that motion keeps moving under prefers-reduced-motion.
