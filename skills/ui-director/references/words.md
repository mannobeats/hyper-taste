# Words

Read this before writing any interface text. Copy is where "generated" shows first, and on a
page that sells it's half the design. Most of the fix is writing less, and writing it plainly
about this one product.

## Headlines that sell

- **Claims, not slogans.** A headline that could sell any product in the category ("Good design
  leaves a mark.", "Stay curious.") says nothing. Say what only this product does, with a number
  or a noun from its world.
- **Specific can still be clever.** A fact with a turn stops people: "The lock thieves walk
  past." beats both "Security, reimagined." and "A smart lock with a 110 dB alarm".
- **Write five, keep one.** For the first screen, write five headlines in different shapes (a
  plain statement, a number, a question, a promise, a scene) and keep the one that is true only of
  this product and still makes someone look twice.
- **Vary the shape across the page.** Two clipped fragments ("Same stack. A third of the bill.")
  once a page at most; the rest are plain statements, questions or numbers.
- **The line under a headline** says how, and for whom, in one sentence.
- **Small lines count too:** captions, notes beside a heading, the footer. If a line gives no
  fact, cut it ("A night well travelled." is decoration).

## Plain, specific, human

Never: elevate, seamless, unleash, revolutionize, next-gen, cutting-edge, game-changer, empower,
supercharge, unlock, robust, leverage, delve, curated, crafted, "in today's fast-paced world",
"take your X to the next level", "all-in-one platform".

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Revolutionize your invoicing workflow | Get paid without the awkward follow-up |
| We take pride in our attention to detail | We fix the small stuff too |
| Leverage AI-powered insights | See which clients pay late |
| Oops! Something went wrong. | We couldn't save your changes. Try again. |
| Success! Your changes have been saved! | Changes saved |
| Are you sure? Yes / No | Delete "Demo"? It can't be undone. Cancel / Delete project |

- **Buttons** name the action in 1–3 words: "Order Lumo", "Send invoice", "Delete project".
  Never "Submit", "Click here" or "Learn more" alone.
- **One label per intent** across the page, nav included.
- **Don't repeat what's already on screen.** Under a "Votes" heading the list is "Last 10",
  not "Last 10 votes". The button names what actually happens ("Claim rewards", not "Earn
  tokens").
- **Friendly beats formal on marketing pages. Precise beats friendly inside the app.**

## Casing and decoration

- **Sentence case everywhere:** headings, buttons, labels, tabs, menus. Never Title Case or ALL
  CAPS.
- **Labels are nouns:** "Revenue", not "Your total revenue for this period".
- **No subtitle under every heading,** no greetings, no "This chart shows…". If something needs a
  sentence of explanation, redesign it first.
- **Steps are named with their verb:** "Install", "Connect", "Send", not "Step 03".
- **Punctuation:** periods and commas. No long dash-joined sentences, no exclamation marks on
  success messages, typographic quotes (“ ”) and apostrophes (’).

## States

- **Empty:** one line on what goes here, plus the button that fills it.
- **Errors** say what happened and what to do, in active voice, next to the field.
- **Destructive confirmations** say what will be lost: "Its 14 files are removed for everyone."

## Real content

Placeholder content makes a design look fake before anyone reads it.

- **No lorem ipsum.** Write real copy for the real product.
- **Names** varied, believable and local to the product's place. Never "John Doe", "Jane Smith",
  "Acme".
- **Numbers** plausible and varied ($1,850.00, 12 days, 3.2 sessions), never 100/200/500, one
  format per kind of number. Invented numbers on a real product are marked as sample data or
  listed for the user to confirm.
- **Dates** vary and fall in a sensible order relative to today.
- **Quotes:** 1–3 lines with a name, a role and one specific detail.
